What this site is and is not.

This site is information, not financial advice

Everything here describes the past and present behaviour of a Flare validator and FTSO provider. None of it is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and none of it is an offer or a solicitation. Past reward rates do not predict future ones.

Delegating and staking carry risk, including the risk of earning nothing for an epoch. The risks specific to this provider are set out on the FAQ and risk page. Decide for yourself, and only with funds you can afford to have illiquid.

Accuracy and availability

Figures are read from public Flare sources and republished automatically. They can be delayed, incomplete or wrong if an upstream source is wrong or unreachable. Each block on the site states where its numbers came from and how old they are; where a source is down, the page says so rather than showing a stale number as current.

Nothing here is a commitment that the service will remain available, or that the validator's staking period will be extended.

Verify before you act

Confirm every address against the block explorer and an independent tracker before sending a transaction. No operator will ever need your seed phrase or private key.

Privacy.

What this site collects, and what it does not.

No accounts, no tracking, no analytics

This site has no accounts, no login and no comment system. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or fingerprinting script. Nothing you do here is recorded by the operator, and no wallet is ever connected to the page.

Preferences you set — the reward currency, for instance — are stored in your own browser and never sent anywhere.

Third parties your browser contacts

Pages are served by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address and user agent in order to deliver them. The site also loads, from your browser:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for typefaces.
  • Coinbase Exchange (api.exchange.coinbase.com) for the FLR market price.
  • Flare public RPC, Flare Systems Explorer, FlareMetrics and the oracle daemon API for protocol data.

Each of these sees your IP address because your browser requests from them directly. The operator receives nothing from these requests and sets nothing in them. Their own privacy terms apply to what they do with that connection.

Contact and corrections

Questions about this page, or a figure you believe is wrong, can go to the verified profiles linked from the home page. There is no mailing list and no contact form, so nothing to unsubscribe from.
